A straightforward format

Before, during,
and after.

Every session is a chance to meet, learn about a store, and decide what you’d like to explore next.

01BEFORE THE SESSION↘

Get to know the store

Stores share the useful details ahead of time.

  • Website and product category
  • Shipping locations and delivery estimates
  • Featured products and current offers
02DURING THE SESSION↘

Meet the people behind it

See what the store makes and ask about what matters to you.

  • Store introduction and brand story
  • Product presentation and discussion
  • Buyer questions and next steps
03AFTER THE SESSION↘

Explore on your terms

Take the conversation wherever you want it to go.

  • Visit the store and browse products
  • Follow the store for future discoveries
  • Purchase directly from the store, if you choose
